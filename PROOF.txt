================================================================================
5. KNOWN LIMITATIONS
================================================================================

 1. No MIME/header parsing. Addresses only; §3.6 fields, Received, Date and
    Message-ID grammars are out of scope.
 2. No message body or MIME multipart parsing.
 3. No §4.5–§4.7 obsolete message syntax (obs-date, obs-received, etc.).
    Only §4.4 addressing productions are implemented.
 4. No semantic/DNS validation: no MX lookup, no existence checks.
 5. Domain-literal contents are not interpreted. "[IPv6:...]" is validated as
    dtext only; IPv4/IPv6 well-formedness is NOT checked.
 6. No IDN/IDNA or SMTPUTF8 (RFC 6531) support. Input must be ASCII; non-ASCII
    is rejected.
 7. Length limits are an extra semantic pass, stricter than the raw ABNF.
    The pure grammar alone would accept longer inputs.
 8. Group "normalized" output is canonical, not byte-identical to input.
 9. `email.utils.parseaddr` is used only in tests, to document divergence —
    it is not part of the implementation.
